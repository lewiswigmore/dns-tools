About DNSTools

DNSTools is a privacy-focused, client-side web interface for DNS lookups. It connects directly from your browser to DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) providers, ensuring your queries are encrypted and never logged by this application.

DNS Queries via:

  • • dns.google/resolve (Primary)
  • • cloudflare-dns.com/dns-query (Fallback)
  • • dns.quad9.net/dns-query (Fallback)

Privacy First Architecture

  • 100% Client-Side: All logic runs locally in your browser.
  • No Backend Tracking: We do not store or see your queries.
  • Secure Transport: Uses HTTPS for all DNS requests.
