Session Information

What is a Session?

A session represents your current active period using DNSTools. It starts when you first open the dashboard and persists as long as you remain active.

What is it used for?

  • Tracks your query volume and success rates for the current day.
  • Calculates average response times from DNS providers.
  • Persists indefinitely until you clear your browser data.

Your session data is stored locally in your browser and is never sent to our servers.
